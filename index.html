FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the build process, requirements, and deliverables.

How long does a project take? ＋
Our standard turnaround time is 7 to 10 days once the project brief is fully approved and assets are provided. If you have an urgent deadline, you can opt for our Priority Delivery add-on to receive your complete live site in 3 to 5 days.
Do I need to provide content? ＋
Yes, you provide your basic text copy, details about your coaching programs, and any personal photography. However, don't worry if it's not perfect—every project includes website copy assistance to help structurally edit and refine your draft text into punchy, high-converting blocks.
Can you help with hosting? ＋
Absolutely. End-to-end launch setup is included. I will handle deploying your custom website to global high-performance cloud hosting on Vercel, connect your custom domain name, and configure full secure routing (HTTPS) completely free of charge.
What's included? ＋
The core package includes a custom-designed, mobile-responsive multi-page website (up to 5 pages) built with performance-optimized code. It fully integrates a high-intent contact form, booking software wrappers (like Calendly), fundamental SEO structure, and complete production setup on Vercel.
Can I request changes? ＋
Yes, a dedicated full revision round is included in your main package. Once the initial build draft is ready, you'll review it and provide a comprehensive feedback list to ensure every detail matches your specific branding goals perfectly.
